Student Mentor

Wigner Summer Camp 2026

Department of Computational Sciences | HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics

At the 2026 HUN-REN Wigner Summer Camp, I mentored two high-school students on a week-long research project investigating time series anomaly detection using the ALTx Python package. After providing foundational training in Python and data analysis, we guided the students in applying adaptive law-based data transformations to peak detection tasks on artificial datasets. The project culminated in a closing seminar where the students presented their findings on whether classical Bayesian Hyperparameter Optimization performs better on original or transformed data.

7 July 2025 - 11 July 2025

Student Mentor

Wigner Summer Camp 2025

Department of Computational Sciences | HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics

At the 2025 HUN-REN Wigner Summer Camp, I mentored high school students through a week-long research project focused on time series data analysis and anomaly detection within the Data and Compute Intensive Sciences Group. We introduced students to the mathematical foundations of time series classification (TSC) and our institute's adaptive law-based transformation (ALT) method using Python and benchmark datasets. The week concluded with a final seminar where students presented their findings on how ALTx-transformed data performs across various real-world and synthetic databases.
