About Me

I am Levente Pocsay, AIS student, Research Trainee and Student Ambassador. I'm currently starting my third semester in the Bachelor's programme "Artificial Intelligence Solutions" at the University of Applied Sciences Upper Austria, while also working remotely as a Research Trainee on a time-series analysis project for the HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics, which has been an ongoing project for almost three years. I am most curious about medical and transportation-related applications of AI. Another topic in my interest is AI's place in primary and secondary school education, including raising students' attention for risks and drawbacks, while also showing how may they use it for their own advantage.

I am fluent speaker of Hungarian and English, while learning German.
